## 🎯 YOUR ACTION ITEMS (Complete These Today!)

### 1️⃣ Submit to Google Search Console (30 minutes)

**Why**: This is THE MOST IMPORTANT step to get your site indexed by Google.

**Steps**:

1. **Go to Google Search Console**
   - URL: https://search.google.com/search-console
   - Click "Start now" button
   - Sign in with your Google account (use your business Gmail: teamwebin3@gmail.com)

2. **Add Your Property**
   - Click "Add property" button
   - Choose **"URL prefix"** method (easier)
   - Enter: `https://www.web-in.co.za`
   - Click "Continue"

3. **Verify Ownership**
   - Choose **"HTML file"** method (easiest)
   - Download the verification file (e.g., `google123abc.html`)
   - Place it in your `public/` folder
   - Commit and push to GitHub
   - Vercel will deploy it automatically
   - Click "Verify" in Google Search Console

   **Alternative**: Use "DNS TXT record" method if you have access to your domain DNS settings at your domain registrar.

4. **Submit Sitemap**
   - Once verified, go to **"Sitemaps"** in left sidebar
   - Enter: `sitemap.xml`
   - Click "Submit"
   - Full URL should be: `https://www.web-in.co.za/sitemap.xml`

5. **Request Indexing**
   - Go to **"URL Inspection"** in left sidebar
   - Enter: `https://www.web-in.co.za/`
   - Click "Request indexing"
   - Repeat for these pages:
     - `https://www.web-in.co.za/pricing`
     - `https://www.web-in.co.za/about`
     - `https://www.web-in.co.za/contact`
     - `https://www.web-in.co.za/work`

**Time**: 30 minutes
**Priority**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ CRITICAL
